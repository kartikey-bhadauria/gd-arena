The Problem

You've never actually sat in a GD. Then you walk into a real one.

A GD needs 6–8 people, a moderator, a good topic, and someone honest to give feedback. Most students get their first real GD in a placement round — where it counts.

1

No Real Panel to Practice

Getting 6 friends together for mock GDs never works: someone jokes around, nobody acts like an aggressive competitor, and timing collapses.

2

Dishonest & Vague Feedback

Peers say "you spoke well" while actual placement recruiters ruthlessly eliminate 80% of the room for lack of structure, weak entry, or passivity.

3

Zero Repeat Drills

Unlike coding problems on LeetCode, you cannot retry a GD 5 times in one evening to master interruption recovery and opening hooks.

Scoring Philosophy

We don't hand out passes.

GD Arena is strict on purpose. We judge behaviour, language, and reasoning patterns — not intelligence. If you're not ready, we'll tell you.

Weak (0–4)

Cut in Round 1

Hesitant entry, zero references to peer arguments, or ungrounded contradictions.

Average (4–6)

Survive, Not Stand Out

Good points but passive participation; failed to take initiative or summarize.

Strong (6–8)

Shortlisted

Structured initiation, composed rebuttals against Aarav, and clear domain grounding.

Elite (8–10)

Lead the Room

Set the conversational framework, defended points with metrics, and drove consensus.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the other participants real people?
No, all other participants (Aarav, Priya, Rohan, Neha, Mr. Verma) are autonomous AI personas with specialized behavioral instructions. This provides a 100% judgment-free environment to practice without social anxiety.
Does it work if my microphone is denied or unavailable?
Yes! GD Arena automatically activates a fast keyboard input fallback bar if microphone permissions are denied, allowing you to practice structured text debate.
Can I interrupt the AI participants while they speak?
Yes! GD Arena features real-time voice barge-in. When you start speaking or hit "Interrupt", the AI instantly yields audio and records how gracefully you handled conversational collision.
Is the feedback really honest or generic praise?
The scoring engine does not auto-pass candidates. If you hesitate, give shallow buzzword answers, or fail to engage with peer arguments, the scorecard explicitly flags it with timestamps and quotes.
Which languages and accents are supported?
GD Arena natively supports Indian English (en-IN) and Hinglish phrasing, using Microsoft Neural voices and Web Speech API tuned for campus placements.
How long does a session take?
You can select 5, 8, or 15-minute GD rounds. 1-on-1 Mock Interviews are structured into 8 progressive questions taking approximately 15–20 minutes.
